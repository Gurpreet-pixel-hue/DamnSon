ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT 
 
This Instruction Kit is designed as a step-by-step guide to assist the user in filling up the webform. This document 
provides references to law(s) governing the webform, guidelines to access the application, instructions to fill the 
webform at field level and important check points while filling up the webform along with other instructions to fill 
the webform. 
User is advised to refer to the respective instruction kit for filing of webform. 
 
This document is divided into following sections: 
 
 
Part I – Law(s) governing the webform 
 
Part III – Instructions to fill the webform 
 
 Part IV – Key points for successful submission 
Click on any section link to refer to the particular section.

PART I – LAW(S) GOVERNING THE WEBFORM 
 
Pursuant to Section 4, 7, 12, 152 and 153 of Companies Act, 2013 read with the Companies (Incorporation) Rules, 
2014. 
 
1.1 
Purpose of the webform 
 
The webform “SPICe + Part B” aims to simplify the process of incorporation of a company and its registration with 
the Registrar of Companies (RoC). The following services can be availed by the user through this webform: 
 
i. 
Application for issuance of Corporate Identification Number / Registration of Company with Registrar of 
Company 
ii. 
Apply for license (only applicable for section 8 companies) 
iii. 
Application for allotment of Director Identification Number (DIN) / Registration of Director 
iv. 
Application for PAN / TAN allocation 
v. 
Application for allotment of GSTIN 
vi. 
Application for allotment of ESIC number 
vii. 
Application for allotment of EPFO number 
viii. 
Application for allotment of PTEC / PTRC (For Maharashtra, Karnataka and West Bengal only) 
ix. 
Application for registration of Shops and Establishment - Delhi / Mumbai 
x. 
Request for Bank Account Opening 
 
1.2 
Important Check Points while filling up the webform 
✓ Please read instructions and guidelines carefully before filling online application forms. 
✓ Please attach the required mandatory supporting documents in the specified format only. 
✓ Please ensure that applicant of the webform is registered at MCA portal before filing the webform. 
✓ Please ensure that the SPICe + Part A application shall be approved and available in the application 
history of the user who filed the SPICe + Part A application (In case SPICe + Part A and SPICe + Part B 
are filed separately). 
✓ Please ensure that the signatories have an approved DIN or valid PAN or valid Membership number. 
✓ Please ensure that If ‘Private (OPC)’ is selected in the SPICe + Part A webform, the PAN / DIN entered 
for the subscriber details shall not match with PAN / DIN of subscriber of any active ‘Private (OPC)’ 
company. 
✓ Please ensure that If ‘Private (OPC)’ is selected in the SPICe + Part A webform, the PAN / DIN entered 
for the nominee details shall not match with PAN / DIN of nominee of any active ‘Private (OPC)’ company.

✓ In case type of company selected is ‘Part 1 LLP to Company’, please ensure that the total number of first 
subscribers having DIN and not having DIN shall match with the total number of designated partners and 
partners in the LLP. 
✓ In case type of company selected is ‘Part 1 LLP to Company’, please ensure that the DIN of individual

✓ Please ensure that no DIN has already been allocated based on the details such as ‘Applicant Name’, 
‘Father’s name’, ’DOB’ entered by the Subscribers cum Directors (not having DIN) and Directors (not 
having DIN) in the webform. 
✓ Please note that DIN provided for Subscribers cum directors/ directors shall not be already associated with 
20 or more companies. 
✓ Please ensure that in case SPICe + Part A and Part B has been filed separately by the user, the SRN of 
SPICe + Part A shall not have been withdrawn/ expired. 
✓ Please ensure that in case SPICe + Part A and Part B has been filed separately by the user, no SRN of 
SPICe + Part B is already pending for processing against SRN of SPICe + Part A. 
✓ Please note that DIN provided for Subscribers cum Directors and Directors shall not be already associated 
with 20 or more companies. 
✓ Please note that in case ‘Class of company’ selected in SPICe + Part B is ‘Public’ then DIN provided for 
Subscribers cum Directors and Directors shall not be already associated with 10 or more public 
companies. 
✓ Please ensure that DIN of the director signing the webform is not flagged for ‘disqualification’. 
✓ Please ensure that the DSC attached in the webform is registered on MCA portal against the 
DIN/PAN/Membership number as provided in the webform. 
✓ Please note that the signing authority of the webform shall have valid and non-expired/non-revoked DSC. 
✓ Please note that in case of resubmission, application of the SPICe + Part B shall be available in the 
application history of the user and T+15 days (where T is the date of marking the application as 
‘Resubmission Required’) should not have elapsed. 
✓ If the space within any of the fields is not sufficient to provide all the information, then additional details 
can be provided as an optional attachment to the webform. 
✓ Please check for any alerts that are generated using the “Notifications and alerts” function under the ‘My 
Workspace’ page in the FO user dashboard on the MCA website.
